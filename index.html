  1. 01

    EthiCook

    On Google Play

    Android app · Kotlin

    Never let any food go off again! EthiCook tells you in time when something is about to go off, and suggests recipes to make the best of it. Just scan the barcode and expiration date, done. It also comes with a nice widget that lets you see at any time what is expiring first. Optionally, you can also contribute to OpenFoodFacts.org (note: I love the service, but I am not affiliated with it).

    Exists because a piece of chicken in the fridge called me by my first name.

    • 200 recipes built in, ranked by what you have
    • Nine languages
    • Free. No ads, no subscriptions, no “premium”
    • Private, and works mostly offline

    Website Google Play

  2. 02

    Magnum BI

    Early alpha · pin a version

    Open-source business intelligence · Rust

    Dashboards a whole company can use: fast, simple, and safe to share between teams and customers. One installation serves many organisations, each with its own people, data and look, and nothing leaks from one to another. Run it as a lightweight server for distributed and enterprise environments, or as a local desktop application: same features, minus the ones that only make sense with other people around.

    Exists because I wanted to test a great database built by a friend, ScramDB, and I ended up building a BI tool out of it.

    • Runs on the cloud, Windows, Linux, macOS, and probably the Amiga 500 (the latter is just an assumption)
    • ScramDB, PostgreSQL, BigQuery, Parquet/CSV/Iceberg via DuckDB
    • Multi-tenant, row filters that only ever narrow
    • Passkeys, SSO, and guest links that expire

    Website Source Desktop app

  3. 03

    Drum Trainer & Metronome

    Coming soon to Google Play

    Android app · Kotlin

    A metronome that listens. A timing coach hears whether you're early or late, through the microphone or exactly over USB from an e-drum, with 26 step-by-step lessons from the first beat to full grooves. Then add your favourite songs from MIDI files or drum tabs and practise along with them, while the coach listens and tells you how you did.

    Exists because music teachers are great but expensive, metronomes are cheap but don't engage you.

    • Real notation, every common time signature
    • Per-limb practice; per-pad timing on e-drums
    • USB MIDI kits: every pad timed exactly
    • Your own songs from MIDI files and drum tabs, with a focus mode
    • Seven languages

    Website

  4. 04

    Attaché

    Beta · not independently audited

    Linux privacy tool · Rust

    An encrypted folder that asks which program may open your files. Every access gets an Allow once / Always allow / Deny prompt, keyed on the calling binary. The vault locks itself when idle, at logout and before suspend.

    Exists because well, I just have fun with cryptography.

    • Built on gocryptfs
    • Per-binary whitelist travels inside the vault
    • Exports to disc with the tools to open it

    Source Releases

  5. 05

    Inkswitch

    Tested on exactly one tablet

    Linux driver & settings app · Rust

    A replacement driver for XP-Pen drawing tablets, with profiles that switch by themselves depending on which application is in front: Krita, GIMP, Blender, anything.

    Exists because the vendor driver and I had creative differences.

  6. 06

    exfat-grow

    0.1.0 · back up first

    Linux disk utility · Python

    Grows an exFAT filesystem in place, without reformatting and without copying your data elsewhere first. A wizard asks before every step that writes anything.

    Exists because on the internet you can find solutions to enlarge many things, but apparently not exFAT filesystems.

    • Single file, standard library only
    • Field-tested: 466 GiB → 954 GiB, data intact

    Source

  7. 07

    Grilled Phish

    In the lab · not released

    Browser extension · JavaScript

    Catches look-alike scam links, like paypa1.com, a Cyrillic pаypal.com or paypal.com.secure-verify.xyz. They get a red box, they don't open when clicked, and the warning highlights exactly which letter is lying to you.

    Exists because I'm getting old, and a big red flag over a scam link is less expensive than new glasses.

    • All look-alike checks run on your computer
    • Recognises copies of the 100,000 most visited sites
    • Blocks websites registered in the last few days
    • Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox

    No link yet. It's still on the grill.
